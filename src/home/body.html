How it works

Open it. Jar it. Seal it. Run it again.

Tabs in NetRelish don't just pile up and disappear. They go into Jars. When the work is done you seal the jar, and everything inside is kept exactly as it was, with a timestamp and a summary.

jar: Friday invoice run

sealed      Tue Nov 17 · 4:12 PM
tabs        7, each in its own room
integrity   untouched since sealing
summary     3 client invoices, payments,
            this week's time sheet
recipe      "Send invoices" · ⌘R
ask         Run it now?
→
Jars hold a project's tabs. Each tab is isolated, but they can pass notes through the jar.
Seal preserves every tab in its original state, timestamped and tamper-proof.
Ferment brings sealed info back to life, so it can feed a recipe or answer a question.
Open a sealed jar later and NetRelish asks if you want to run the recipe it was made for. Press ⌘R and the work happens again.

Buying
How you get itFree downloadIn-app purchase

Pro is an in-app purchase on the Mac App Store, billed to your Apple Account. Manage or cancel it in System Settings, and use Restore Purchases in NetRelish Settings on a new Mac. If you stop paying, you keep every jar and seal. Only the Pro features switch off.

Questions

Before you buy.

Why the Mac App Store?

It's the safest way to install a browser. Apple reviews it, sandboxes it and handles updates. Buying Pro uses the Apple Account you already have, with no new login and no card form.

Does Pro work on more than one Mac?

Yes. Pro follows your Apple Account to every Mac you sign in to, and Family Sharing extends it to your household.

What does "tamper-proof" mean for a sealed jar?

When you seal a jar, NetRelish records each tab exactly as it was, with a timestamp. If anything in the jar changes afterward, the seal check shows it.

Does my browsing go to the cloud?

No. Your Jars, Brine and Pantry live on your Mac, and Ask the Pantry runs on Apple's on-device models.
